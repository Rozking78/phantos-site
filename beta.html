Become a beta tester

Phantos is a show-control console for macOS — lights, media and cameras through one merge. Before it ships, a small group of people run it on real rigs. If you work shows and you are willing to tell us what breaks, apply below.

01What you are signing up for

Read these four before you fill anything in. If any of them is a problem, this is not the right time to join — and that is a perfectly good answer.

It is unfinished

Pre-release software with known and unknown defects. It will misbehave. Never put it in front of an audience without a tested fallback you can switch to.

Never for anything that can hurt someone

No rigging, hoists, automation, flying, pyro, lasers or life-safety systems — ever, and that is a condition of using it, not advice.

Data sharing is required

Testing sends us a log of the commands you issue — including ones you speak aloud. It is how the beta works, so it is not optional. You can see exactly what would be sent before it goes.

You can say you are testing

Tell people you are a Phantos beta tester if you like. What stays private is the software itself — builds, screenshots, video, defects, roadmap and pricing.

02How it works

Five steps. Nothing is automatic — a person reads every application.

1 · You apply

Accept the agreements below and send us your details.

2 · We review

We read it and decide. You will hear back either way.

3 · You get a key

Approved testers get a licence key by email, and an invitation to a private folder with the build in it. There is no download link on this site — the folder is how the build reaches you, and how your reports come back.

4 · You install

Up to three machines. Enter the same key on each. Phantos Launcher goes on every Mac you use.

5 · You renew

A beta key is a full licence for one month. Phantos warns you in good time before it lapses; press Request renewal in the Launcher and we issue a new one. Nothing switches off if it does lapse — Phantos keeps running and simply asks you to get in touch.

03The agreements

Both apply. Read each one to the end — the checkbox unlocks when you reach the bottom. They are not long, and they are written to be read.
