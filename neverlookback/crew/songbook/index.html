Load lyrics

On Ultimate Guitar, open a song, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C, and paste it here. Paste as many songs as you like, one after another. Each one is cleaned up and matched to its song by title. Check the list, then press Load.

To see and select song files: press Import .md. Choose one or several .md / .markdown files; the picker shows all files. Or drag the Never Look Back charts folder here. Scan folder selects a whole folder and reads its charts after you press Select Folder; that dialog only shows folders.

A loaded chart replaces that song's current chart.
