What it is

Everything that’s normally a separate purchase

Lighting console

Programmer, presets, groups, executors, FX, align and fan, per-channel priority merge. DMX, sACN, Art-Net.

Media server

Renders canvas to displays and NDI. Content, gobos, crossfades, warp and edge-blend, multi-node.

Camera + switcher control

VISCA and PTZ, Panasonic AW, ATEM, Roland, PJLink — with a dedicated camera priority tier.

Voice control — works offline

Speak console commands and they execute — on-device speech, no internet required, gated by an explicit safe word. Natural-language asks and deeper AI faculties are in development. AI never sits in the render path.

Call it on comms

Phantos rides its own intercom channel. Key the channel from your beltpack, call the cue like you’d call it to an operator — end with the safe word and it executes. The headset you already wear is the console.

Key decks + game controllers

Stream Deck surfaces run native — command keypads, executor grids, encoder dials with live feedback. Pair a game controller and drive positions, PTZ and zoom with your thumbs.

The defensible part isn’t any one faculty. It’s one merge arbitrating lights, media and cameras together — which nobody else does.
