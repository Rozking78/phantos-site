What it is

Five categories that are normally five purchases

Lighting console

Programmer, presets, groups, executors, FX, align and fan, per-channel priority merge. DMX, sACN, Art-Net.

Media server

Renders canvas to displays and NDI. Content, gobos, crossfades, warp and edge-blend, multi-node.

Camera + switcher control

VISCA and PTZ, Panasonic AW, ATEM, Roland, PJLink — with a dedicated camera priority tier.

Voice + AI — in development

Natural-language control gated by an explicit safe word — nothing actuates without it. Camera and device-learning faculties are being built and proven now. AI never sits in the render path; every show runs with it off.

The defensible part isn’t any one faculty. It’s one merge arbitrating lights, media and cameras together — which nobody else does.
